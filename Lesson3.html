Why Email Still
Dominates

  • Direct channel ownership — unlike social media, you own your email list and aren't subject to algorithm changes.

02a · Crafting Compelling Content

Words That
Convert

  • Subject lines under 50 chars — get to the point; curiosity gaps and numbers drive open rates up to 22%.
  • Preheader text — the second subject line; use it to complement and extend the subject line's hook.
  • One email, one goal — don't dilute focus. Each email should have a single clear call-to-action.
  • Mobile-first copy — 60%+ emails opened on mobile. Short paragraphs, scannable structure.
  • Storytelling — lead with a narrative. People connect with stories before they connect with products.
  • Urgency & scarcity — "24 hours left" and "only 5 spots remaining" drive immediate action.

04a–b · Deliverability & A/B Testing

Reach Inboxes,
Not Spam

📬
Deliverability Checklist
  • Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records
  • Warm up new IP addresses gradually over weeks
  • Clean list regularly — remove bounces and inactives
  • Maintain spam complaint rate below 0.1%
  • Avoid spam trigger words: "FREE!!!", "CLICK NOW", "100% guaranteed"
  • Always include a visible unsubscribe link
🔬
A/B Testing Framework
  • Test one variable at a time — subject line OR send time, never both
  • Minimum sample size of 1,000 subscribers per variant for statistical significance
  • Run tests for at least 24–48 hours to account for time-zone variation
  • Use a 90% confidence threshold before declaring a winner
  • Document results and build a testing roadmap from learnings
04c · Compliance with Regulations

Legal Foundations
of Email Marketing

CAN-SPAM
US federal law governing commercial email. Applies to all commercial messages sent to US recipients.
  • No deceptive subject lines or sender information
  • Must include physical mailing address
  • Clear opt-out mechanism in every email
  • Honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days
GDPR
EU regulation requiring explicit consent and data transparency for all EU residents, regardless of sender location.
  • Require explicit, affirmative consent to subscribe
  • Right to access, correct, and delete personal data
  • Data breach notification within 72 hours
  • Document all consent with timestamps and source
CASL
Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation — one of the world's strictest. Applies to emails sent to or from Canada.
  • Requires express or implied consent before sending
  • Consent expires after 2 years without renewal
  • Clear sender identification required
  • Penalties up to $10M CAD per violation
Best Practices
Universal ethical standards that build subscriber trust regardless of legal jurisdiction.
  • Preference center for frequency and content control
  • Easy one-click unsubscribe in every email footer
  • Privacy policy linked from sign-up forms
  • Regular consent audits and database hygiene
