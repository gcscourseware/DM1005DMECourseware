The Content-Market Fit Test
Ask three questions before creating anything:

1. Does my audience actually search for this?
2. Can I add unique value beyond existing content?
3. Does this serve a business goal (awareness, leads, retention)?

If all three are yes → build it.
Topic Scoring Framework
  • Search volume — is there monthly demand?
  • Keyword difficulty — can we realistically rank?
  • Business relevance — does it reach buyers?
  • Unique angle — do we have something original to say?
Creating High-Quality Content
The Anatomy of
Content That Performs
  • Original research & data — primary sources, surveys, and experiments that can't be found elsewhere.
  • Expert quotes & validation — interviews and citations from recognized authorities increase credibility and sharing.
  • Comprehensive depth — cover the topic fully. Thin content loses to thorough content every time in search.
  • Strong opening hook — the first 3 sentences determine whether anyone reads the rest. Lead with value or curiosity.
  • Visual hierarchy — headers, bullet points, callouts, and images that reward scanning before deep reading.
  • Clear, singular CTA — every piece needs an obvious next action that advances the reader's journey.

Repurposing Content
One Idea, Ten Formats,
Maximum Reach
The Pillar Content Model
Start with one long-form pillar piece (a comprehensive guide or original research report) — then systematically break it into smaller format-specific derivatives for every platform.
  • Long-form blog → 5 short posts — each section becomes a standalone article with its own SEO value.
  • Blog → Video script — adapt written content into a YouTube or TikTok explainer.
  • Data points → Infographic — visualize key stats for Pinterest and LinkedIn shares.
  • Article → Podcast episode — narrate and expand with guest perspectives.

The Three-Channel
Distribution Framework
Owned Media
You Control It

Your website, blog, email list, podcast feed, and YouTube channel. Invest here first — zero distribution cost, full control, compounding returns.

Earned Media
Others Share It

PR coverage, organic social shares, backlinks, influencer mentions. Earned media is the most credible but least predictable channel.

Paid Media
You Buy Reach

Sponsored social, content amplification platforms (Outbrain, Taboola), and paid search. Use to accelerate owned media's reach on key pieces.

Distribution Best Practices
Publish, then promote. Most brands spend 80% of time creating and 20% distributing. Flip this ratio — spend equal or more effort on promotion. A great piece seen by no one drives zero results.
  • Community distribution — share in relevant Slack groups, forums, LinkedIn groups, and subreddits with authentic participation.
  • Content syndication — republish on Medium, LinkedIn Articles, and industry publications for extended reach.
  • Outreach & link building — notify people mentioned in your content; they often share or link back.

Calendar Building Principles
Themes over topics. Organize monthly themes around business priorities, then fill in specific topics within each theme. This creates strategic coherence across all channels.
  • Publishing cadence first — commit to a realistic frequency you can sustain, then scale up.
  • Lead time buffering — keep 2–3 weeks of content in the pipeline at all times.
  • Seasonal alignment — map content to industry events, product launches, and search seasonality.
  • Cross-channel coordination — align blog, social, email, and video around the same weekly theme.
